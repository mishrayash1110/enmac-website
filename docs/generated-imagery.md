# ENMAC card imagery

Created using the built-in image generation tool. These are illustrative scenes, not photographs of ENMAC offices, employees, customers, properties or partners. The existing ENMAC logo is unchanged.

Website assets (WebP, 1200 × 800):

- `public/images/enmac-advisory.webp`: financial consultation; personal finance, guidance and documentation cards.
- `public/images/enmac-home.webp`: residential interior; home and property finance cards.
- `public/images/enmac-business.webp`: manufacturing and distribution workspace; business, working capital, corporate and trade finance cards.

## Final prompts

All prompts use the `photorealistic-natural` category, landscape 3:2 framing, realistic editorial photography, natural daylight, restrained ENMAC-compatible navy and pale blue accents, authentic textures and imperfections. No logos, legible text, watermarks, floating charts, CGI or illustration.

### Advisory

An Indian financial consultant and a small business owner quietly reviewing papers at a modern but ordinary office desk. Candid, unposed, slight side angle, naturally resting hands, laptop screen turned away, blank unbranded document backs. Soft window daylight, realistic skin texture, slight natural imperfections, restrained navy and pale blue clothes, warm wood and off-white office. Main subjects toward right side, airy desk on left for overlay text. Real photographic optics, 50mm lens, restrained contrast. No advertising smiles, handshake, money piles, glowing charts or holograms. Illustrative imagery, not actual ENMAC staff.

### Home

Believable lived-in restrained interior, not a luxury mansion or CGI real estate render. Empty room, no people, no keys or coins. Normal eye height, straight believable architecture and subtle shadows, 35mm lens, realistic photographic detail and slight imperfections. Central-right visual focus and calmer left area for optional text.

### Business

No people. Slate blue, navy, natural grey and warm paper palette, soft realistic daylight, 35mm lens at normal eye height, straight perspective with authentic material texture and small imperfections. Equipment and shelves toward right, calmer left foreground. No labels, fake data, symbols, floating charts or excessive symmetry.

## Readability

`CardBackdrop` renders an empty-alt decorative image under the original HTML content. Card text and controls remain fully opaque. CSS applies reduced image opacity, desaturation and a separate light or navy overlay. Guide artwork uses a stronger image treatment because it contains no body copy. Form inputs, calculator results, disclosures and branding remain unobscured.
